About me

Learning languages has shaped how I teach.

I am a TESOL master's student with a background in linguistics and experience teaching and tutoring language learners.

My path into TESOL

My academic work in linguistics, second-language acquisition, and sociolinguistics has strengthened my interest in how language learning creates connection and opportunity. I have taught learners with varied language backgrounds, ages, and proficiency levels, including in university language programs and in Thailand.

As a multilingual speaker, I understand that learning another language can bring both confidence and uncertainty. I want my classroom to be a place where learners can practice, ask questions, make mistakes, and recognize their progress.

Teaching interests

I am especially interested in communicative language teaching, academic language development, vocabulary learning, reading instruction, and supportive assessment. I aim to design lessons that connect language goals with meaningful tasks and provide different ways for learners to participate.

Professional goals

I am continuing to develop as a reflective TESOL educator. I hope to support multilingual learners as they pursue personal, academic, and professional goals, and to keep learning from students, colleagues, and research.
