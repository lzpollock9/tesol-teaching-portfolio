Course description

Learners build reading ability through comprehension work, strategy instruction, vocabulary learning, extensive reading, and fluency practice. Texts include level-appropriate narratives and short structured passages.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand level-appropriate texts, including main ideas, important details, author purpose, and point of view.
  • Use previewing, skimming, scanning, and inference to make meaning.
  • Use context and common affixes to infer unfamiliar vocabulary.
  • Read aloud with appropriate rate, pausing, intonation, and pronunciation.

Assessment overview

  • Reading comprehension tests: 40% of proficiency assessment
  • Reading strategy quizzes: 20%
  • Vocabulary quizzes: 15%
  • Fluency and rate: 15%
  • Oral reading: 10%

Learning experiences include reading logs, practice assignments, projects, and collaborative tasks. Course policies and internal contact details from the source syllabus are not reproduced here.

Sample course materials

Reading Explorer 1 (third edition), Stargirl, Shoe Dog: Young Readers Edition, a paper or notebook, and a pencil or pen.
