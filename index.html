Welcome

A reflective, learner-centered approach

My teaching is informed by second-language acquisition, sociolinguistics, and experience working with learners from different linguistic and cultural backgrounds. I value clear learning goals, supportive practice, useful feedback, and classroom relationships that make it easier to take linguistic risks.

This portfolio brings together my teaching philosophy, professional experience, course planning, and selected classroom materials.
